      chapter 3 · LIMEN · 1st place
      
97% accuracy, on a microcontroller, no cloud

      
LIMEN is a wearable that predicts panic attacks before they happen. The LSTM reached 97% validation accuracy. The hard part was running it in real time on an nRF5340: quantized to int8, with an IMU filter against false alarms.

      
On-device by design: someone seconds from a panic attack can't wait for a network round-trip, and their data stays on their wrist.

      chapter 4 · HERALD · CTO · May – Sep 2026
      
Drone localization without GPS, 1–2 s per frame

      
HERALD estimates a drone's position by matching its camera feed against a satellite mosaic (132 tiles, 3072×2816 px, 0.73 m per pixel). Brute force meant 9,628 windows and ~12 minutes per frame. Three stages cut it down.

    

    

      

        
brute force9,628 windows · ~12 min

        
global pass132 windows · ~13 s

        
local + momentum~19 windows · 1–2 s

      

      
SuperPoint / SuperGlue matching gives 70–150 inliers per match in open terrain, 3–5 in dense cities. Validated on MovingDrone sequences up to 104 frames, with pySLAM visual odometry tracking 15/15 frames; ~5 s per frame on average in simple scenarios.
